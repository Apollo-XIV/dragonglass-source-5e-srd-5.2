## Changes

This is not the original document. The SRD 5.2 text and data were converted by
[5etools-to-dragonglass](https://github.com/Apollo-XIV/dragonglass-5etools-converter) from 5etools'
SRD data into [dragonglass](https://github.com/Apollo-XIV/dragonglass-builder) content
elements: reorganised into JSON, with structured mechanics (resources, modifiers, spell lists
and so on) added where they could be read from the text.

Some mechanics in this content were derived, with changes, from the structured data of the Foundry VTT dnd5e system (https://github.com/foundryvtt/dnd5e).
